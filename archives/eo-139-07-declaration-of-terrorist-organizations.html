EO 139-07 Declaration of Terrorist Organizations

Issued by President mypenjustbroke on 10 April 2025.

Whereas: SimDemocracy has suffered at the hands of terrorist organizations.

Whereas: the President has a responsibility to declare and condemn the actions of such terrorist organizations.

§1. The organizations listed in this Executive Order shall hereby be designated as Terrorist Organizations.

§2. Such organizations are Golden Consciousness, the Proletarian Unity Party, and the Traditionalist Insurgency for Defense and Enforcement.
