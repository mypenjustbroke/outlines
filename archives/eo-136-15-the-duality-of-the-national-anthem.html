EO 136-15 The Duality of The National Anthem

Repealed by National Pride Act 2025

Issued by Muggy (._.symph0ny._.)

Whereas No piece of existing legislation mandates the official SinDemocracy National Anthem.

Whereas Two anthems that are of members of the SimDemocracy Community should be recognized

#The formal official anthem of SimDemocracy shall be “Democracy’s Triumph” (Link: https://youtu.be/Vx8nWBuzTxw?si=n5B_4uFEKZR7r5SI) and shall be used in formal events or ceremonies. #The informal official anthem of SimDemocracy shall be “Stand Up Damned of Reddit”(Link: https://youtu.be/TsNWNpbCA7A?si=kAVKh3zaZULjqDMF) and will be informally used within casual events and gatherings.
