Consultation and Feedback Act

Repealed by the Rules of the Senate

An Act to create avenues of feedback for larger bills.

Article 1: Feedback §1. There shall be a forum channel within the SimDem Discord Server Senate Category for the purpose of soliciting feedback from members of the public, which Senators shall be able to post their bills to.

§2. There shall be a role within the SimDem Discord Server named "Public Consultations" which may be assigned to any person who selects it from Server Onboarding, which is mentionable by the Speaker of the Senate, who shall ping the role whenever a bill for public consultation is posted by any Senator.

§3. The Speaker may refer any bill for Public Consultation before it is debated on.

Authored by hmquestionable

Passed by the Senate on 19 March 2025

Signed by the President on 19 March 2025
