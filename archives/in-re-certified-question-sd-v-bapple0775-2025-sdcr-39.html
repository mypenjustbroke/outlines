In re Certified Question SD v bapple0775 [2025] SDCR 39

VERDICT by Judge Hmquestionable

(with Judge Ppatpat and Judge Brandmal agreeing)

Question [1] This is a certified question from a lower court in relation to the case [[SD v Bapple 2025 Crim 135|SD v bapple0775 [2025] Crim 135]]:

“If a witness is no longer available, contactable or the testimony cannot be obtained (specifically mid-testimony), how should the trial proceed?”

Response [2] The Constitution affords every person the right to a fair hearing. When only one party is able to question a witness, the hearing is no longer fair. The testimony should therefore be excluded.
