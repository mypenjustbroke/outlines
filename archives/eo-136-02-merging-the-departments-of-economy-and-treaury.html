EO 136-02 Merging the Departments of Economy and Treaury

Issued by ._.symph0ny._.

Whereas there is unnecessary confusion when it comes to the duties of the Department of the Treasury and Economy

Whereas there is bloat when accounting for the paychecks of the departments staying separate.

1. The Department of Economy and Department of the Treasury will merge together, under the name of the Department of the treasury.

2. The current employment of the Department of Economy will be transferred to the treasury.

3. The current Secretary of the Treasury will remain the Secretary of the Treasury under the merging of the departments.

4. The current Secretary of Economy will become the Deputy Secretary of the Treasury
